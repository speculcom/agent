适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

已在用 IDE 且希望不切换工具就能用 Claude 的人。 需要在终端、IDE、GitHub 三处用同一工具的人。 重视权限可控性与 MCP 生态的人。

不适合需要开源许可或自托管的场景—— 许可明确为 proprietary。 也不适合要求「权限行为在 IDE 与 CLI 严格一致」的场景，本次未核验两者是否完全一致。

People already in an IDE who want Claude without switching tools. People who want one tool across terminal, IDE and GitHub. People who care about permission control and the MCP ecosystem.

Not a fit for scenarios needing an open-source licence or self-hosting — the licence is explicitly proprietary. Also not a fit where permission behaviour must be strictly identical between IDE and CLI; this round did not verify whether they match.
