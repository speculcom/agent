适合与不适合When to use it, when not to

不适合需要完全免打扰的自动化场景—— 执行命令前询问会打断流程，需确认 YOLO 模式的具体开关方式。

Users who need switchable multiple providers and who care about confirmation before bash commands run. Participants in the ACP ecosystem.

Not a fit for fully unattended automation — asking before each command interrupts the flow; the exact switch for YOLO mode was not verified.
